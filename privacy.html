Privacy Policy

Police Chase Simulator: Santa's Revenge · Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Police Chase Simulator: Santa's Revenge ("Police Chase", "the game") handles, and why. The game is made by Otto Kling ("I", "me").

The short version

  • There are no accounts. I don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Your progress (coins, cars, best score, missions and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage information to show, measure and improve those ads, as described below.
  • You choose whether ads can be personalised, using Apple's tracking prompt and, where the law requires it, Google's consent form.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your coins, the cars and upgrades you own, your best score, mission progress and settings (such as sound, music, power-up button side and Christmas mode) on your device. This information is not sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads. It shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to continue a run or double your coins) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.

When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect the following:

  • Device identifiers. This includes Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when the game asks. It also includes identifiers limited to this app or developer.
  • Approximate location. This is worked out from your IP address. The game does not ask for or use your precise location.
  • Advertising data. For example, which ads you have seen.
  • Product interaction. For example, app launches, taps and ad video views.
  • Diagnostics. Crash logs and performance data, such as launch time and energy use.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how they perform, provide analytics, prevent fraud, and improve its services. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalised based on your activity in other apps and websites. If you don't, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised that way.

To learn more, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: The first time you play, iOS asks whether the game may track you. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your device.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): Where the law requires it, Google's consent form appears before ads are personalised. You can change your choices at any time using Privacy Choices in the game's Settings (the gear on the main menu).
  • Ad personalisation: You can also limit ad personalisation in your Apple account settings.

Children

Police Chase is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact me and I will help remove it where I can.

Data sharing and selling

I don't collect personal information from you, and I don't sell or share any. Information collected by Google through ads is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, I'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
